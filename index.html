About Minty Health Organization

Minty Health Organization is a nonprofit that provides primary care, preventive services, and health education to families in our area. We run three neighborhood clinics and hold regular screening days at libraries, churches, and schools.

Our staff includes physicians, nurses, counselors, dietitians, and community health workers. Fees are based on income, and no one is turned away because they cannot pay.

Before Your Visit

What to bring

Photo ID, your insurance card if you have one, and a list of any medicines you take.

New patients

Please arrive 15 minutes early to fill out forms, or complete them ahead of time in the patient portal.

Cost of care

Our sliding fee scale is based on household size and income. Ask at the front desk or call billing.

Need to cancel?

Call your clinic at least one day ahead so someone else can use the appointment.
